Elastomer Conference

ILCEC 2027 on DD–DD Month 2027
in Seoul, Republic of Korea

The International Liquid Crystal Elastomer Conference (ILCEC) will be held in 2027 at the Royal Hotel Seoul, Myeongdong, in the heart of Seoul, Republic of Korea. Following ILCEC 2025 in Tampere, ILCEC 2027 will bring together chemists, physicists and engineers to share fundamental advances and application opportunities for this exciting class of materials. The conference will cover, but is not limited to, dynamic and multiresponsive LCEs, soft mechanics and actuation, 4D printing, feedback-driven and autonomous motion, tunable photonics, and novel behaviour and functions of LCEs. Contributions on related stimuli-responsive soft materials are also welcome. We hope you will consider joining us in Seoul!

Venue

The conference takes place at the Royal Hotel Seoul (61 Myeongdong-gil, Jung-gu) in the heart of Myeongdong, Seoul's most vibrant shopping and dining district, next to Myeongdong Cathedral and a few minutes' walk from Myeongdong and Euljiro 1-ga subway stations. Seoul is a city of 10 million where 600-year-old royal palaces and hanok villages sit beside a global hub of semiconductor, display and soft-materials research. Gyeongbokgung Palace, Bukchon Hanok Village, Namsan and the Cheonggyecheon stream are all within easy reach of the venue.

Getting there

Incheon International Airport (ICN) is linked to Seoul Station by the AREX express train (about 45 min); the venue is then one subway stop or a 10-minute taxi ride away.

Accommodation

A block of rooms at a conference rate will be pre-reserved at the Royal Hotel Seoul. Myeongdong offers a very wide range of hotels and guesthouses at every price level within walking distance of the venue; details will follow in the 2nd announcement.

Social program (tentative)

Welcome reception on Day 1
A welcome reception will be held at the conference hotel on the evening of the first day.

Conference dinner on Day 3
A Korean-style banquet in the city centre, with a traditional performance.

Cultural afternoon on Day 4
Guided visit to Gyeongbokgung Palace and Bukchon Hanok Village, or a Han River evening cruise.

Topics

  • · Synthesis, alignment and processing of LCEs and LC networks
  • · Photo-, thermo-, electro- and magneto-actuation; soft robotics
  • · Soft mechanics, instabilities, defects and topology
  • · 4D printing and microfabrication
  • · Feedback-driven, autonomous and self-oscillating motion
  • · Tunable photonics, stretchable and wearable devices
  • · Theory, modelling and data-driven design
